# United States rail package — sources and method

`us-2025.json` is a fail-closed `compact-v1` package. Its 250 published lines
use 182 route-isolated government/operator GIS geometries, 43 official GTFS
shapes that pass an independent track cross-check, and 25 NARN routings. It
contains no station-to-station fallback chords.

## Authority order

1. A reviewed operator or government railway centreline, stored as a
   route-isolated GeoJSON with exact publisher, URL, raw SHA-256, and normalized
   SHA-256 provenance.
2. The operator's GTFS for route/station identity, station order, service
   pattern, and published colour. GTFS geometry is accepted only after the
   independent FRA/OSM comparison and final geometry gates.
3. FRA/BTS North American Rail Network routing for mainline services, using
   the operator publication as the service corridor and station-order source.
4. OpenStreetMap is a visual/track cross-check matching the WebUI basemap. It
   is not used to override a conflicting reviewed official GIS centreline, and
   no final US line is sourced directly from OSM.

The reviewed official source families include MTA subway and LIRR, CTA,
NORTA, LA Metro, SFMTA, Sound Transit, VTA, MassGIS/MBTA, SEPTA,
MARTA/Atlanta, Miami-Dade, Maryland iMAP/MTA, WMATA/DCGIS, Pittsburgh PRT,
Houston METRO, DART/NCTCOG, Detroit People Mover, CAGIS Cincinnati, Utah UGRC,
Metra/City of Chicago/RTA, Metropolitan Council, NJ Transit/PATH,
Metrolink/SCAG, FDOT Rail LRS, FRA/BTS Amtrak route GIS, and Oregon Metro RLIS
for Portland Streetcar route 194. Exact endpoints and hashes are in the build
manifest and package
`verifiedOfficialNetworks` metadata.

## South Shore Line geometry review (2026-08-31)

The Lakeshore Corridor uses NICTD's current `Lakeshore_Shape` from static GTFS
feed version `2026_Monon_v3` (SHA-256
`a96c8634ce69393626751f027dd444a120b9052d221a27fa2e982a4b81fda120`).
The older NARN routing predates the rebuilt Hammond Gateway approach and
reaches 48.98 m from the independent OpenStreetMap rail reference in the
Hegewisch–Hammond Gateway interval, so that interval was previously withheld
from both clients. Across all 390 operator-shape vertices, the current NICTD
alignment measures 1.25 m median, 4.30 m p95 and 32.04 m maximum from the same
independent track reference, with no unmatched vertex. NICTD documents that
the Double Track project opened on 2024-05-14 and that Hammond Gateway replaced
the former Hammond station on 2024-10-18.

The Monon Corridor remains on NICTD GTFS geometry: its published full corridor
is within 5.4 m of the current `Monon_Millennium_Shape` except for the audited
136 m straight platform lead-in at Munster/Dyer, which the independent package
cross-check confirms on active rail. Metra Electric remains on the City of
Chicago KML whose raw SHA-256 is unchanged; all four decoded parts have zero
unmatched vertices and maxima of 19.01 m, 12.59 m, 9.40 m and 12.52 m against
the independent FRA/OSM reference.

## Fail-closed rules

- Missing or invalid official-network provenance blocks that route; no GTFS or
  NARN fallback is allowed for a route configured to require the official GIS.
- A failed trunk suppresses every generated branch.
- Collinear densification does not hide an endpoint chord.
- Internal near-reversals, broken seams, station-order jumps, repeated
  non-loop station calls, and unresolved station anchors are release blockers.
- A government layer documented as converted from GTFS cannot independently
  verify the same GTFS.
- A provenance-verified operator/government centreline remains visible when a
  lower-authority visual cross-check disagrees. The exact intervals and
  deviation remain audit warnings; only GTFS/NARN candidates without that
  primary-source authority are withheld from display.
- Colours must be the exact GTFS `route_color` or an exact registered official
  route palette. Random/default/generated/fallback colours are rejected.

The WebUI and iOS renderers retain round line caps and joins; build-time
grooming also preserves curved track instead of replacing it with schematic
station connectors.
