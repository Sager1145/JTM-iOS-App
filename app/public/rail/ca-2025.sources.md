# Canada rail package — sources and method

`ca-2025.json` is built by the same fail-closed pipeline as the United States
package. Its 49 published lines use 21 route-isolated government/operator GIS
geometries, 18 official GTFS shapes that pass an independent track
cross-check, and 10 NARN routings. It contains no station-to-station fallback
chords and no line sourced directly from OSM.

Canadian reviewed spatial sources include City of Calgary LRT centrelines,
City of Edmonton LRT tracks, TransLink's official system GIS, Toronto/TTC
subway shapefiles, Ontario's ORWN Track network, and Québec MTQ railway GIS
where an operational feature explicitly identifies VIA passenger use.

GO Transit publishes seven ORWN-routed geometries: Barrie, Lakeshore East,
Lakeshore West and its Hamilton branch, Milton, Richmond Hill, and Stouffville.
The output coordinates come from Ontario ORWN; GTFS supplies station identity,
order, service, and colour. Kitchener and UP Express remain blocked for
near-reversals in the available official routing.

exo is blocked because the official geometry download could not produce
reproducible bytes and a SHA-256. STM is blocked because its published
shapefile is documented as converted from GTFS shapes/stops. REM remains
blocked without an independently surveyed centreline. TTC 306 and 506 are
blocked as complete route families for unresolved station/geometry conflicts.

Cross-border station ownership is country-specific: Niagara Falls `NFL` stays
on the US side, `NFS` stays on the Canadian side, and Saint-Lambert has one
Canadian identity. No coordinate is inferred merely from a station name.

Names, time zones, route colours, and station order come from each operator's
publication. See [`us-2025.sources.md`](./us-2025.sources.md) for the shared
authority order and release rules, and
[`na-2025-line-review.md`](./na-2025-line-review.md) for every published and
blocked route.
