## Confirmed corrections

- Alaska Fairbanks and Whittier use guarded station overrides; Aurora Winter
  is approximately 571 km rather than the erroneous 1,065 km geometry.
- Densified collinear station chords are detected geometrically and rejected,
  regardless of whether metadata calls them GTFS, NARN, or OSM.
- A failed trunk suppresses all of its branches. No orphan branch is shipped.
- `(In)` and `(Out)` are directional suffixes; New Orleans Canal 47 is an open
  25-stop line rather than a doubled loop.
- Hudson and Maple Leaf use reviewed station order; cross-border Niagara and
  Saint-Lambert identities are country-correct.
- NCTD uses official GTFS `reference_place` identities. Unparented same-name
  grouping is limited to a 200 m complete-link cluster, so TTC and SFMTA stops
  cannot merge through a proximity chain.
- TTC 306 is blocked for an unresolved 255 m same-identity station-anchor
  disagreement. TTC 506 is blocked because its GTFS branch differs by 294.45 m
  from the independent track cross-check and no independent TTC streetcar
  centreline is available.

## Still blocked

The strict national-completeness gate remains closed. Examples include BART,
WMATA, Cleveland, DART, Detroit, Cincinnati, Milwaukee, CATS, DC Streetcar,
Pittsburgh PRT, exo, REM, STM, UP Express, and individual official routes with
disconnected or reversing geometry. A government layer explicitly derived
from GTFS is not accepted as an independent second source. Exact route-level
reasons are in the line ledger and build report.
