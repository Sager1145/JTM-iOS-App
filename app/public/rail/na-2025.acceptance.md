# North America passenger-rail acceptance status — 2026-09-01

This is a fail-closed release, not a claim of national completeness. A service
is omitted when its station order, identity, colour, alignment, provenance or
redistribution status cannot be supported without guessing.

## Display rendering — 2026-09-02

Both clients now draw every `us`/`ca` railway as ONE continuous stroke per
chain of intervals (`app/public/rail-stroke.js`, `RailCore.ContinuousStroke`,
held to the same answer by `port-fixtures/continuous-stroke.json`, 31 cases).
Nothing is cut at a lane change any more: a lane change is a smoothed drift
baked into the geometry at the current zoom, corners are rounded to
`strokeCornerRadiusPx`, and each platform bead is the offset of its own vertex.

| Web features for the 250 US lines | 519 (87 lines in several pieces, up to 15) | 250 |
| Web features for the 66 Canadian lines | 80 | 66 |

| Amtrak Carl Sandburg vs Metra BNSF stroke separation at z15, Chicago–Aurora | weaving, crossing lanes | median 2.70 px (exactly one lane), p90 2.70 px |

The lane table (`display-lanes.json`) keeps its rows and gains
`followsByRegion`; withheld intervals still break the stroke, and a blocked
line's rows are keyed per display part so they never bridge the gap.
Regression: `python3 -m unittest scripts.railway.tests.test_display_network`
(19 tests), `swift test --filter ContinuousStrokeParityTests`, and
`ios/verify.sh --core` (285 tests) pass.

## Confirmed corrections

- A missing release policy now defaults to `strict`; invalid policy values are
  rejected. A route configured with a verified official network cannot fall
  back to GTFS geometry when that network fails.
- The feed cache fingerprint includes the official-network manifest, so a
  provenance or normalized-geometry change invalidates stale cached output.
- Every display vertex is checked against an independent FRA/NARN, OSM or
  reviewed official centreline. Display limits are 20 m for street rail, 25 m
  for metro, 30 m for commuter, 35 m for regional and 50 m for long-haul.
  Over-limit GTFS/NARN candidates remain withheld; a provenance-verified
  operator/government centreline remains visible and records the lower-authority
  reference disagreement as a warning. This restores 68 visible intervals,
  including CTA Blue Line at Division–Damen and Belmont–Addison.
- Failed official branch routing can no longer fall back to a different NARN
  path. This removes five false Metro-North branches, while explicit source
  priority removes a lower-priority spatial-subset Cascades publication.
- Official-network anchors snap to the nearest point on a segment and route
  through temporary projection nodes. Sparse GIS vertices no longer force
  station anchors to a distant endpoint.
- Northeast official integrations now cover reviewed WMATA, MTA subway, LIRR,
  MBTA, SEPTA and Pittsburgh PRT routes. Oversized DC Streetcar, WMATA Silver,
  MBTA Red, LIRR Port Washington, MTA Q and PRT Blue/Red results are explicitly
  withheld instead of silently falling back.
- Eastern Canada adds reproducible MTQ exo geometry, NRWN Kitchener and UP
  Express routing, and Toronto track geometry for TTC 306. STM, REM and
  unresolved TTC routes remain fail-closed.
- Mid-South additions include reviewed DART/NCTCOG, Detroit People Mover,
  Cincinnati Connector, Metra Rock Island and Houston Purple Line geometry.
  QLINE, Milwaukee Hop and CATS Gold remain blocked where independent geometry,
  station reachability or official colour is unresolved.
- Portland Streetcar route 194 (A Loop) now uses a route-isolated Oregon Metro
  RLIS centreline. The other nine TriMet/Portland declarations remain
  fail-closed pending their own topology or evidence reviews.
- Providence/Stoughton now selects the operator-published Back Bay/Northeast
  Corridor as its primary trunk. Stoughton and the timetable-published
  Fairmount diversion remain separate physical branches rather than being
  spliced into one false main line.
- Reviewed shared corridors now make Amtrak and local services use one physical
  centreline where the station pair and independent railway evidence agree.
  Five currently blocked intervals have a verified replacement. The South
  Shore Chicago approach now passes its reviewed-track checks; the distinct
  Lakeshore interval from Hammond Gateway to East Chicago remains withheld at
  its 49.0 m independent-reference disagreement, and PATH at WTC remains
  withheld after its endpoint check failed.
- NICTD Lakeshore and Monon now inherit the reviewed Metra Electric physical
  centreline from Millennium to Kensington Interlocking. Their own geometry is
  retained beyond the exact junction, so Metra main, Blue Island Branch and
  South Shore remain three real directions rather than a gap or false branch.
