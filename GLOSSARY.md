# Railway journeys

The language used for recorded journeys, physical routes and timetable evidence.

## Language

**Passenger choice**:
A physical railway path explicitly selected by the passenger. A filled route section alone is not evidence that the passenger selected it.
_Avoid_: Authored choice when referring to an automatically filled section.

**Corridor preference**:
A suggested physical corridor inferred from the recorded journey. It guides route resolution without asserting a passenger choice.
_Avoid_: Confirmed route.

**Package inference**:
A route proposal inferred from the railway package's available station intervals. Uniqueness within that package does not establish complete physical topology.
_Avoid_: Passenger choice, physical proof.

**Ridden journey**:
A journey whose record explicitly says that at least one stretch was ridden. A past date alone does not make a journey ridden.
_Avoid_: Completed journey when completion is inferred only from the clock.

**Route gap**:
A requested stretch or connection with no proven drawable physical route.
_Avoid_: Straight-line connector.

**Source geometry**:
The physical railway path used as evidence for a resolved journey.
_Avoid_: Display geometry when discussing physical route evidence.

**Display geometry**:
The railway curve prepared for drawing, including visual smoothing and shared-corridor lane placement.
_Avoid_: Physical connection when referring only to visual continuity.

**Station complex**:
A group of associated railway stations or passenger facilities. Membership alone does not prove a shared track, platform or traversable railway connection.
_Avoid_: Platform when referring to the entire group.

**Operator station**:
A railway station in the context of a particular operator. It can belong to a station complex without being interchangeable with another operator's station there.
_Avoid_: Station complex when identifying a passenger's specific railway station.

**Passenger platform**:
The particular passenger boarding facility, distinct from a station complex, access passage, yard or nearby railway.
_Avoid_: Station footprint when claiming a specific boarding face or its usable extent.

**Review provenance**:
The record of how a conclusion was investigated and supported. It identifies the review and its evidence, not the physical railway itself.
_Avoid_: Source track identity when referring only to a review reference.
