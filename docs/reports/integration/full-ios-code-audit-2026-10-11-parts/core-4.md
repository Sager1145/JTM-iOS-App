No ownership/consumed marker is recorded. `rawStops` at :184-188 passes the copied clock into `assign`; :295-296 interprets that clock as 09:03 arrival at the last station, even though the copied marker says 発 and no destination arrival exists. The wrong arrival can consequently be saved or supplied to timetable completion/matching; no observed matching result, mileage or runtime behavior is claimed.

Preserve the intended following-row preference and explicit arrival/departure semantics.
