## 肥薩線: distinguish suspended north from operating south

JR Kyushu's [current disaster notice](https://www.jrkyushu.co.jp/railway/notice/) and [current operating-notice fragment](https://www.jrkyushu.co.jp/trains/info/inc/info_mid.html), retrieved October 11, explicitly retain the 八代–吉松 suspension. Correction on temporal integration: the [June 22 postponement](https://www.jrkyushu.co.jp/common/inc/news/newtopics/__icsFiles/afieldfile/2026/06/22/260622_hisatsu-line_infomation.pdf) superseded that plan; the [June 29 final announcement](https://www.jrkyushu.co.jp/news/__icsFiles/afieldfile/2026/06/29/260629_hisatsu-line_2.pdf) set July 12, and [Kirishima City](https://www.city-kirishima.jp/tiikiseisaku/kotu/comebackhisatsusen.html) confirms service actually resumed that day. 八代–吉松 remains suspended. A current linked [southern maintenance notice](https://www.jrkyushu.co.jp/common/inc/kouji/__icsFiles/afieldfile/2026/02/20/20260220_oshirase_hisatu_line_1.pdf) lists specific daytime cancellations on October 21, November 25 and December 23, rather than continuous southern suspension.

The compact line `jp-九州旅客鉄道-肥薩線` currently has `serviceStatus: partial_service_suspended` and `serviceSpans: [[0,18,1]]`. Local station index 18 is 吉松 `009944` [130.738135,32.011865]; indices 16/17 are 矢岳 `009928` [130.76982,32.10427] and 真幸 `009932` [130.72911,32.074525]. Thus the recorded historical 矢岳→真幸 risk lies in the officially suspended northern passenger section. This is a scope inference from the official endpoints and the local identities; it does not prove rendering/date-filter interpretation of the service-span code.

Retain that historical alignment defect as unresolved for historical/suspended display. It should not be presented as an operating-passenger track alignment acceptance case on October 11. The operating southern section remains independently eligible for risk review, and these sources do not certify its geometry. The older bilingual 2025 suspension PDF still found by search says 吉松–隼人 reopening unknown; the explicit dated 2026 reopening and current notice supersede that old status. No removal or geometry repair is authorized by this operating-status finding alone.

The official image shows two **longitudinally staggered side platforms**, one on each outside edge of a curved two-track corridor; their drawn platform bodies do not overlap longitudinally. Diagram-left platform is labeled びわ湖浜大津方面, diagram-right platform 三条京阪・太秦天神川方面. Two labeled crossings lie in the drawn gap between platform bodies. Each platform has its own gate/ticket-booth area; a municipal toilet lies at the inner end of the diagram-left platform access. The note says access to the 三条・太秦天神川-bound platform is stairs only. This is a passenger-platform and entrance relationship, not a scaled physical survey.
