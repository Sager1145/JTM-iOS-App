# V1 rejection

The original `candidate.json` / `candidate.geojson` proposal is retained as historical proposal evidence and is **REJECTED**.

The V1 review treated source indexes 2 and 6 as valid rail-locus guards and estimated only ±2.5 px selection uncertainty. Full-resolution review shows that assumption was false. In point-2, the own labelled Meitetsu rail centre is about `(908,165)` while source index 2 projects to `(908.09,189.35)`. At `x=837`, the own centre is about `y=203` while the old source polyline is about `y=232`. V1 therefore follows the south edge/corridor through a 24–29 px visible error on the eastern bridge approach. Its ±2.4 m statement described an uncertainty around the wrong feature and must not be used.

V2 is isolated in `v2/`; no V1 coordinates or reprojections were overwritten.
