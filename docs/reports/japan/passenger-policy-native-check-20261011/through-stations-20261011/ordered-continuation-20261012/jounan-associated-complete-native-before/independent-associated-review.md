# Jōnan associated-record independent native review

Date: 2026-10-13

This is a read-only visual review of the 43 associated-record viewpoints. I
viewed every panel in `review-0.jpg` through `review-14.jpg` (43 basemap,
43 display, and 43 smoothed panels; 129 panels total), then inspected the saved
full-resolution Main pairs for the full city network, west junction, and Dōgo
terminal. Capture counts establish receipt completeness only and are not an
acceptance result.

## Concrete visual findings

- **No renderer break in the associated records.** Across 城北線 `#0–#7`,
  大手町線 `#0–#3`, 本町線 `#0–#3`, 花園線 `#0`, and 連絡線 `#0`, the displayed
  and smoothed strokes remain continuous within each interval. Adjacent views
  preserve their endpoint direction. I found no zero-length edge, isolated cap,
  new V-cusp, or reverse-U introduced by smoothing.
- **The west-junction misalignment is still concrete.** 大手町線 `#0`
  (`001595/000000–000001`) places the horizontal cyan/red stroke along the north
  side of the broad Apple tram guide. The focused Main west-junction pair makes
  the displacement legible: the Otemachi/Nishihoribata horizontal stroke is
  about 15 m north of the Apple guide/frame band, while the north–south stem is
  about 6 m west. The broad contact panels do not override that focused evidence.
- **The serialized Jōnan backtrack is not cured by the associated network.** The
  related-line panels show smooth, individually plausible arms, but they do not
  turn the Jōnan ordering 本町一丁目 → 西堀端 → 南堀端 into an operational
  through movement. Reading those legal intervals as a single train path still
  creates the previously documented west-then-east reversal.
- **本町 line preserves a distinct arm.** 本町線 `#3`
  (`001602/000000–000002`) approaches 本町一丁目 on the north–south arm and
  does not visually collapse that stop onto the west-arm 西堀端 position.
  Likewise, the two line-specific 本町六丁目 faces remain visibly separate in
  the broad views. The images provide no basis to merge either shared-ID pair
  to one display coordinate.
- **花園 and 連絡 lines are continuous but remain independent legal records.**
  花園線 `#0` (`001617/000000–000002`) follows the north–south Hanazono corridor
  into its endpoint bends without a cap. 連絡線 `#0`
  (`001618/000000–000001`) follows the visible east-loop curve between its two
  shared junction identities without a reversal. These shapes do not by
  themselves authorize a Jōnan↔花園 or Jōnan↔連絡 physical continuation.
- **Dōgo endpoint shows no separate short-tail defect.** In the focused Main
  Dōgo pair, the Jōnan endpoint reaches the visible Dōgo Onsen station frame
  without a hook, detached dot, or zero-edge. The full-network Main pair also
  shows the city lines connected at overview scale, but that scale cannot prove
  stop-face precision or operational route continuity.

## Scope disposition

The associated capture set is complete and visually usable, but it is not an
acceptance baseline for a repair. The current result remains **WARNING** because
the focused west-junction offset is visible and the legal-arm question remains
an identity/topology issue. Any owner candidate still needs the complete
six-record/29-interval scope already listed in `repair-scope-prep.md`, plus
focused evidence at 西堀端/本町一丁目, 南堀端, 上一万/平和通一丁目, 古町, and the two
本町六丁目 stop faces. Physical rail records, station IDs, route ordering, and
cross-identity junction authorization remain unchanged.

No source data, UI state, build, or candidate geometry was changed for this
review.
