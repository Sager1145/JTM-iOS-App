# V2 z18 review — rejected for refinement

All 17 z18 basemap reprojections were opened. V2 corrected the old south displacement but placed #19 and the first half of #20 on the north rail edge rather than the representative midpoint between the paired thin black rail strokes; #18's later body showed the same smaller bias. V2 remains process evidence and is superseded by V3. No production acceptance.
