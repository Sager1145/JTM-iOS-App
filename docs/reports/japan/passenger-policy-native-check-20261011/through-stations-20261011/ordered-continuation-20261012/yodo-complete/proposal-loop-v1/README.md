# Yodo interval 0 loop proposal v1

Candidate only. No source, physical graph, native app, station alias or production data was changed.

`interval-0-coordinates.json` contains the complete 若井→家地川 proposed display interval (143 coordinates). `interval-0-proposal.json` contains the literal 104-point source, station rows, IDs, retained mileage, 79 exact pixel witnesses, cameras via raw references, palette, signed offsets, source chainage and limits. `reprojections.json` gives every control and guard projection, visible/offscreen status, and exact camera/mapRect for all 18 current Secondary frames. Each `reprojection-NN.png` overlays red literal source, green proposal, magenta witness controls and black retained guards on the actual raw basemap.

Indices 0–60 are unchanged literally. All 61 occur in the current Nakamura source geometry; no source supports after 60 do. Indices 101–103 and both station endpoints are unchanged literally. Only the unshared loop region 61–100 is replaced. IDs, 5.775 km, station aliases and topology remain separate and untouched. The retained shared guard60 is approximately 5 pixels from the thin glyph; this is disclosed rather than changing either owner's shared span. Guard101 is on the evidenced far straight approach; the final station endpoint remains unchanged.

Witness extraction uses exact raw WGS84/WebMercator mapRect at heading/pitch zero, scale8 map points per pixel. It searches the source normal ±60 pixels and requires blue support at five longitudinal positions. RGB predicate B−R>70, B−G>30, G−R>20 rejects the cyan stream and gray road329/buildings. Actual raw views8–17 identify the lower westward Yodo arm by continuous passenger direction; the upper Nakamura arc is excluded with a manual separator in common raw view11. A first unmasked extraction jumped briefly to that competing arc and was rejected; `rejected-unmasked-controls.json` preserves it. The supplied current proposal removes those jumps. Colour alone was insufficient.

Controls are ordered by common projected source chainage, never camera order, spaced about25 m. Each proposed fitted point is the inverse projection of an actual selected integer raw pixel. The largest current turn is16.85°; no modified control triples reverse or turn above30°. One planned witness C069 was unresolved by the five-position persistence test. Its neighbouring actual witnesses C068/C070 span50 m of chainage on the same visible cartographic curve; the proposal connects them directly and records the gap, without substituting a road or station/building point. All18 reprojected frames were actually viewed, with original-size focus9–17 and both guards; no obvious raw candidate hook or camera join jump remains.

The helper `fit-proposal.py` is local to this proposal folder and reproduces these files from the recorded basemaps/current source. It does not invoke AppleMapRailExtractor or alter it; its thin-colour persistence approach is informed by that existing extractor's transitCartography mode. The exact mask and limitations are recorded rather than presented as a general framework.

Thin blue sleeper-marked cartography is the requested guide here. It does not establish surveyed forest/tunnel rails or station platform faces. Reprojections show raw controls; native smoothing, full Main lane−0.5 joins and station-dot placement still require Root's complete candidate replay before acceptance.
