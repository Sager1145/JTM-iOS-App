# Yodo regional corridor identity — bounded primary-source research

Research date: 2026-10-13. Scope: `jp-四国旅客鉄道-予土線`, 若井→北宇和島, all 19 intervals in `scope.json`. This note supplies regional route identity and through-service context, not alignment acceptance or new topology.

## Decisive route evidence

The official JR station/timetable index lists the Yodo passenger sequence, reversed here to match the scope: 若井→家地川→打井川→土佐大正→土佐昭和→十川→半家→江川崎→西ケ方→真土→吉野生→松丸→出目→近永→深田→大内→二名→伊予宮野下→務田→北宇和島→宇和島. Thus the scope’s complete 20-station/19-interval ordering matches the operator’s sequence; 宇和島 is the passenger continuation beyond the scoped endpoint. [JR official timetable index](https://www.jr-shikoku.co.jp/01_trainbus/jikoku/index.html)

The regional legal/route map defines 予土線 as 若井–北宇和島, 76.3km, and 予讃線 as continuing to 宇和島. JR’s Yodo tourism page instead describes the dedicated 北宇和島–川奥信号場 section as 72.7km. Keep these differing extents explicit; neither is grounds to move a station ID or rewrite existing kilometre data. [Shikoku Transport Bureau regional railway map, page 1](https://wwwtb.mlit.go.jp/shikoku/content/000344997.pdf), [JR Yodo page](https://www.jr-shikoku.co.jp/yodo_line/)
