# Johoku complete raw-map independent review

Reviewed 2026-10-13. Scope is `jp-伊予鉄道-城北線` intervals 0–7 only. I opened all 19 original 999×654 `basemap.png` files and their paired `analysis.json` files under `johoku-intervalN-viewM-z18-before/apple-map-extraction/`. Counts were `[2, 2, 3, 3, 2, 2, 2, 3]`. Every image was loaded; the metadata line ID and interval index matched its directory. Temporary source-seed and station-pixel overlays were used only to read the originals and are not evidence artifacts.

The extractor status is `partial_image_support` in every view. Its platform candidates are therefore references for locating pixels, not accepted station representatives. Apple POI matches are also labels, not platform centres.

## Identity findings

- **Heiwadōri-itchōme / 008736:** interval 0 view 0 and view 1 show the Johoku geometry leaving the literal point at about `(500,327)` along the same southeast–northwest diagonal station/curve used by the Renraku approach, then turning west onto the loop corridor. This is the legal shared Heiwa boundary. The thin east–west marks around `(245…290,254)` lie well west, amid repeated `Iyotetsu Loop Line` casing and the approach to Red Cross Hospital; they are not sufficient evidence for moving Heiwa roughly 100 m west. The exact line-scoped representative still needs the independently reviewed normal projection onto the diagonal black glyph. Do not substitute the west casing candidate merely because it is horizontal.
- **Hommachi 6-chōme / 008719:** interval 5 view 1 and interval 6 view 0 put the Johoku point `(500,327)` on the east–west loop rail. The north–south Hommachi dead-end face is a different line-scoped stop. Nothing in these frames supports moving Johoku onto that north–south face. Longitudinal centring within the east–west Johoku stop remains visually weak because the rendered frame/casing is interrupted by labels and the road junction.
- **Furumachi / 008739:** interval 7 views 1–2 show the Johoku approach entering the eastern side of the multi-track Furumachi complex. The current point `(500,327)` is south of the visible platform group. The eastern/right-hand platform strip around the supported candidate near `(543,277)` in view 2 is the plausible Johoku/shared-tram face; the central and western long strips belong to the broader Takahama complex and must not be selected by proximity. The approach identity supports a bounded line-scoped correction, but the exact longitudinal midpoint should remain pending the full Johoku/Renraku guard review because several overlapping strips are visible.

## Scope conclusion

The 19 frames support the Johoku route identity and continuity for all eight intervals. They expose station-representative work at Teppōchō, Takasagochō, Kiyachō, and Furumachi, plus a weaker Red Cross warning. Heiwa must stay on its legal diagonal shared boundary rather than being moved to the west east–west casing; H6 must stay on Johoku's east–west face rather than the Hommachi dead end. Shimizumachi and the exact H6 midpoint remain insufficiently resolved. This is raw cartographic evidence only; it does not accept a candidate, native rendering, Main rendering, or train-passable connection between line-scoped station faces.
