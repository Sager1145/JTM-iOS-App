## Result and limitation

The Third Inspector set shows a continuous Fujigaoka-to-Takabata display route across all 21 intervals. Endpoint tails are complete and adjacent intervals keep compatible travel directions. In particular, `#14` proceeds north/northwest from Nagoya and then west to Kamejima without a reverse U or JR-line borrow, while `#0` uses the diagonal Higashiyama frame rather than the Linimo east-west frame. The Third images establish no concrete geometry repair.

Acceptance is limited to visible native display continuity and own-line cartographic-guide identity. Because most of the route is underground, these Apple blue guides, station casings, and labels do not certify a surveyed tunnel centreline, exact platform midpoint, metre-level offset, or exact curve radius. A future coordinate repair would require independent station or tunnel evidence rather than a road-centre assumption or constant translation inferred from these panels.
