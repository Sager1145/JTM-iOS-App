## Result and limitation

The 21 intervals form one continuous Fujigaoka-to-Takabata display route with complete endpoint tails and compatible adjacent directions. The specific Nagoya-to-Kamejima risk does not show a false reversal or U-turn, and Fujigaoka uses the Higashiyama north-south frame rather than the Linimo east-west frame. No concrete display repair is established by these Primary panels.

This acceptance is limited to visible native display continuity and own-line cartographic-guide identity. The line is predominantly underground; Apple blue linework, station casings, and labels do not establish surveyed tunnel centreline accuracy, exact platform midpoint, or metre-level alignment. Any future geometry change would need independent tunnel or station-frame evidence rather than a road centreline or a constant translation inferred from these panels.
