## Evidence limits

The original pairs inspected above report heading0, pitch0 and `zoom256=17`; all are 1024×768 native raster exports. No Apple-pixel residual or metre displacement was measured. This visual review does not establish whether the disagreement originates in canonical source, display processing, native coordinate handling or Apple cartography. Do not repair by nearest-rail snapping or erase the retained bends.

Interval7 ord13–15 and interval8 ord6–11 show long railway artwork across forest with little visible ground/portal detail. Those hidden or insufficiently identified stretches cannot establish precise tunnel geometry. Roofs, station artwork, indistinct rails and labels likewise cannot establish exact passenger platform selection. Batch smooth images do not certify circles, company lane synthesis, popup membership or production-main appearance; root retains those checks after completing the full line.

No production edit is proposed from these images alone. Prioritize same-leg location evidence for Minami-Daito's two-sided approaches, the Hataya eastern approach and Hinobori's river bend, while retaining the other exact candidates above in the sole checklist's supporting evidence.
